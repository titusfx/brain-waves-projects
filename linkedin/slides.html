Why it stayed broken · 2017 → 2025

Neither bug was the crypto

1 · It picked the wrong key

emokit chooses its AES key with serial.startswith("UD2016") — a literal string test. My dongle is a 2018 unit, so it silently took the wrong key and printed confident garbage. No exception. No warning.

2 · It read the bytes backwards

Every 16-bit sample field was decoded big-endian. They are little-endian. Same offsets, opposite order — which is why it looked like a signal for eight years.

Offsets were right. Byte order was wrong. 3 / 8
